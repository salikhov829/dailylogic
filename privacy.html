Daily Logic — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Developer: Eldar Salikhov (Kazakhstan)

Daily Logic ("the App") is a puzzle app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the App handles. The short version: the App does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.

1. Data we do not collect

The App has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no trackers. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The App does not transmit any data to servers operated by us.

2. Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, results, streaks, statistics and settings are stored only on your device (in the App's private storage). They are not uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the App. If you copy a result to share it, you decide where it goes.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. Apple and Google provide us with anonymised transaction information needed to unlock the features you bought and to handle restores and refunds, under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. To delete all local data, uninstall the App. To manage or cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

6. Changes

If a future version of the App starts processing personal data (for example optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the App's release notes.
